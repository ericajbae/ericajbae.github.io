About

Why some children come to love science

My research focuses on understanding the origins of variability in STEM learning outcomes, with a particular emphasis on identifying contextual, motivational, and cognitive predictors.

Ultimately, my goal is to support children and families from all backgrounds in developing positive and successful learning experiences in STEM — which means looking closely at the settings where curiosity first takes root: the home, the caregiver, the earliest years of school.

Prior to my doctoral studies, I earned an M.Ed. in Child Studies (clinical and developmental research track) from Vanderbilt University and a B.A. in International Relations (with honors) from New York University.

Research interests

  1. Learning motivation
  2. Learning cognition
  3. Learning contexts — especially home & parents
  4. Early & mid-childhood development
  5. Quantitative methods
